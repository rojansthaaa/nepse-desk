Market scan

Every listed stock run through the same 35 indicators.

Signals follow the standard textbook reading of each indicator on the latest bar. The overall call averages the four families equally so that the many trend indicators don't outvote the rest. Information-only rows (ATR, pivots) are not scored. This is a screening aid, not investment advice; indicators lag price, and thinly traded stocks give noisy readings.

How the model call works: every past day for every NEPSE share, the 32 indicator signals were recorded alongside how the share did over the next 10 sessions compared with the market average. A ridge regression then learned how much each signal actually mattered on NEPSE. Short-term breakout and momentum signals turned out to hurt, while oversold oscillators and the medium-term trend helped, so the model favours buying dips inside uptrends. It was checked by fitting it on data before 19 October 2025 and testing it on the year after. Each day's shares are then split into five equal groups from Buy to Sell / avoid. The edge is real but small: a few percentage points of hit rate, which only pays off across many trades.

NEPSE data: built from the exchange's daily floor sheet (every trade) and official closing prices, mirrored publicly by the open-source YoNEPSE project and refreshed here each trading day after the close. Prices are not adjusted for bonus or rights issues, so a big drop on a book-closure date is a corporate action, not a sell-off, and indicators will treat it as a real fall for a few weeks.

Your own data: any CSV with Date, Open, High, Low, Close, Volume. Exports with LTP and Qty columns (common on NEPSE price-history pages) are read automatically, newest-first or oldest-first. 200+ daily bars gives every indicator a value. You can still upload or paste one to analyze something outside the dataset.
